B. Legal Bases for Processing Your Personal Data

We may process your personal data on the legal bases in accordance with the GDPR as follows:

  • Consent
    We may process your personal data based on your consent. We will only process your personal data based on your consent, where we cannot or otherwise choose not to rely on any other legal bases as mentioned below. Where we process your personal data based on your consent, you have the right to withdraw your consent at any time. Should you wish to withdraw your consent please contact us at dpo@terragon.tech. However, please note that any withdrawal of your consent will not invalidate any processing operations carried out prior to the withdrawal of your consent.
  • Contract
    We may process your personal data as may be necessary for the entry or performance of a contract between you and us.
  • Legal Obligation
    We may process your personal data as may be necessary for our compliance with a legal obligation under any applicable laws of the European Union or Member State.
  • Legitimate Interest
    We may process your personal data as may be necessary for our legitimate interests, such as business or commercial interests, or to exercise our legal rights in any legal proceedings. We will only process your personal data on this basis if our legitimate interests are compelling and proportionate when balancing against your rights and interests.

Please note that you have the right to object against our processing of your personal on this basis by contacting us at dpo@terragon.tech. We will consider your objection as soon as reasonably practicable and provide you with an appropriate response.
