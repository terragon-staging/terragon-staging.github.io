What are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files which we store on your computer or other device when you access this website. They are generally required for us to ensure the proper operation of this website, and to provide you with the best user experience possible in accessing and using this website. Cookies typically contain anonymised information and do not include names, email addresses and/or other personal data.

How do we use Cookies?

We use cookies on this website to:

  • ensure the proper operation of this website
  • remember your browsing preferences on this website
  • gather analytics data of the users of this website, including:
    • the number of times a user has visited this website
    • the time a user enters and stays on this website
    • the search engine and keyword used by a user, and where a user accesses this website

There are some cookies which we have to use for the proper operation of this website. For this reason, they do not require your consent.

In addition, we use certain preference cookies to remember your browsing preferences, including whether you have agreed to this Cookies Policy. These cookies are set by us and only we can read them.

Further, analytics cookies are used by us to assess how can we improve the services we provide our users. These analytics cookies gather analytics data on how you interact with this website as an anonymous user. Although we may obtain information about your computer or other device such as your IP address, your browser and/or other information from our use of these analytics cookies, the information which we obtain is anonymised and does not contain your personal data.

You are free to refuse these preference and analytics Cookies, amongst others, by clicking "Accept only essential Cookies" via the banner you see when you visit this website.

How can you manage Cookies?

You can delete all cookies on your computer or other device by clearing the browsing history of your browser. This will remove all cookies from all websites you have visited including this website.

If you do not wish to accept cookies on this website, you can change your browser settings so that cookies are not accepted. If you do this, please be aware that you may lose some of the functionality of this website.

For third-party services, you can withdraw from the tracking activity by utilizing the information provided in the third-party's privacy policy or cookies policy, by clicking the relevant opt-out link (if provided) or by contacting the relevant third-party.

You can also find information about how to manage cookies in your preferred browser by visiting the service provider's website.
